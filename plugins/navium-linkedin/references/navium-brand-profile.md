# Navium Maritime LinkedIn Brand Profile

## Brand
Navium Maritime Shipping Services

## Positioning
Practical maritime consultancy, audit, inspection-readiness, training and competency solutions informed by shipboard experience.

## Audience
Shipowners, ship managers, fleet managers, marine superintendents, vetting teams, crewing managers, Masters, senior officers, training/competency and QHSE professionals.

## Primary topics
SIRE 2.0, RISQ 3.2, marine audits, vetting, crew training, competency management, LMS/CMS, shipboard operations, maritime safety and leadership.

## Content objective
Build professional credibility, educate maritime decision-makers, demonstrate practical expertise and generate qualified conversations without turning every post into an advertisement.

## Voice
Practical, technical, calm, direct, experienced, credible and educational.

## Avoid
Generic AI language, exaggerated claims, unsupported statistics, fake case studies, confidential information, excessive emojis and aggressive sales language.

## Rule
When current regulatory or industry requirements are discussed, verify the current source and distinguish requirements from recommendations or personal experience.
